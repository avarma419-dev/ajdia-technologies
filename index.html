How we work

We work with a small number of ongoing clients on a retainer or project basis. Work is billed by invoice, weekly or per project, and paid securely online by bank transfer or card.

Billing, refund and cancellation policy

Billing. Services are agreed with each client in advance and billed by invoice, weekly or per project. Invoices list the work delivered and are payable online in USD or CAD.

Refunds and disputes. If you believe an invoice is incorrect, or a service was not delivered as agreed, email us within 30 days of the invoice date. We will review it and, where the charge was in error or the work was not delivered, issue a full or partial refund to the original payment method within 10 business days. We aim to resolve every concern directly before a bank dispute is needed.

Cancellation. Ongoing services can be cancelled by either party with written notice by email. Work already delivered up to the cancellation date is billed; nothing further is charged after that date.

We provide services only; no physical goods are sold or shipped.
